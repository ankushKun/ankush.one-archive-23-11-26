Right now I'm building my startup, [Construct Computer](https://construct.computer/?utm_source=ankush.one&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=about_story). It's a workforce of AI employees that each get their own cloud computer, so they can actually finish work instead of just talking about it. Think browser, terminal, files, memory, the whole desk, not another chat window.

That's the latest chapter. Before this I was deep in developer tools, blockchain infrastructure, communities, and a ridiculous number of hackathons. I like building things that ship, breaking stuff for fun, and being the kind of nerd who will stay up all night to make something cool work.

I build developer tools that scale. As an **Entrepreneur in Residence at Forward Research**, I shipped 5+ production-ready developer tools used by 1K+ developers across the ecosystem, including [BetterIDEa](https://betteridea.arweave.net) (acquired), [AO Package Manager](https://apm_betteridea.arweave.net) (now part of AO's core tooling), and [Subspace](https://subspace.arweave.net).

At **Arweave India**, I provided technical mentorship to 100+ developers, helping teams ship blockchain projects, and co-organized 4+ hacker houses to grow the builder community.

Earlier, at **Physics Wallah**, I built an interactive live-class system that processed 10K+ real-time chat messages for classes with 50K+ concurrent viewers.

My journey into tech started long before blockchain. I've always loved experimenting, from building robots and mini-satellites to making objects levitate with sound waves.

Since 2017, I've competed in 50+ hackathons across ecosystems like Ethereum, Solana, Arweave, and more, earning more than a dozen prizes. One of my favorite projects evolved into [Arlink](https://arlink.arweave.net), Arweave's flagship deployment tool that raised a seed round at $2.5M valuation.

In 2023, I won the first Arweave India Hacker House. The story behind it is something I share in my ["TEDx" talk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWW4BCZPIUg).

Outside of coding, I love sharing ideas, experiments, and live builds on my [YouTube channel](https://youtube.com/@AnkushKun). You'll find everything from developer explainers to fun physics demos.
